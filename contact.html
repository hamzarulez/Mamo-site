What happens next. We read the request and reply by phone or email during business hours. If the job needs a site visit, we book a time that works for you. If the power is off or something looks unsafe, call instead of using the form.

What to include in a request.

The details that help us quote the job accurately before we come out.

  • The address, or the nearest intersection if the address is tricky to find.
  • A photo of the panel, or of the problem if it is not a panel job.
  • When the building was last worked on, if you know.
  • Whether the power is currently off, and which circuits are affected.
