The lowest quote is not always the cheapest job.

What a licensed, permitted job includes, and what turns up later when those steps are skipped.

When steps are skipped

The cheap quote

  • No load calculation, so a new circuit can overload the panel
  • No permit and no inspection, which can cost you at resale
  • Undersized wire and breakers nobody labelled
  • No written scope, so extras appear on the invoice
The Ayeza job

Permitted, inspected, documented

  • Load checked before the work is priced
  • ESA permit pulled and inspection booked
  • Correct wire size and every breaker labelled
  • Written scope and price before we start

How a job runs, start to inspection.

The same four steps whether it is one outlet or a full service upgrade.

  1. 01

    Call or send the details

    Tell us what is happening, or send a photo of the panel. We ask the questions that change the price before we come out.

  2. 02

    On-site assessment

    We check the load, the wiring, and the access. You get a written scope and price before anything is ordered.

  3. 03

    Permit and install

    We pull the ESA permit, schedule the work, and keep the power on where the job allows it.

  4. 04

    Inspection and handover

    The ESA inspection is booked, the panel is labelled, and you keep the paperwork for your records.

What finished work should look like.

Clean runs, labelled circuits, and a panel the next electrician can read without guessing.

Questions we get before the first visit.

If your question is not here, call and ask. You will get a straight answer, not a sales pitch.

Do you pull the ESA permit?

Yes. Work that needs a permit gets one, and the inspection is part of the job, not something you chase afterwards.

Are you licensed and insured?

Ayeza Electrical Supplies Inc. holds ECRA/ESA Electrical Contractor Licence #7019157 with the Electrical Safety Authority. Ask for the current certificate of insurance before work starts and we will send it.

What does the quote include?

Labour, materials, the permit fee, and the inspection. If we open a wall and find something that changes the work, we stop and price it before continuing.

How soon can you come out?

Most quotes are scheduled within a few business days. If the job is urgent, call and we will tell you honestly what is possible that day.

Do you work with builders and property managers?

Yes. We handle fit-ups, unit turns, and scheduled maintenance for landlords, builders, and small commercial sites.

Is a permit required for a panel upgrade?

In Ontario a service or panel upgrade needs an ESA permit and inspection. We apply for the permit and meet the inspector on site.

Need a quote or a straight answer about a job?

Call during business hours, send a text, or fill in the quote form. We will tell you what the work actually needs.
