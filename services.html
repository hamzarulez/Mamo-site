What the quote covers.

A written price before work starts, and a clear rule for anything that changes it.

  • Labour for the work described in the written scope
  • Materials, including breakers, wire, and fixtures listed on the quote
  • The ESA permit fee for work that needs a permit
  • The inspection, and the paperwork you keep afterwards

Some jobs change once a wall is open. Damaged wiring, an undersized panel, or a circuit that is not what it looked like from the outside are all hidden conditions. When we find one, we stop, explain it, and price the extra work before continuing.
